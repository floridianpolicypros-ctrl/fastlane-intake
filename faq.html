How many times can I elect traffic school in Florida?

1
election per 12 months
5
elections per lifetime

Those are the two hard limits in Florida law. You cannot elect a basic driver improvement course if you already made an election in the previous 12 months, and you cannot make more than five elections in your entire life.

“However, a person may not make an election under this subsection if the person has made an election under this subsection in the preceding 12 months. A person may not make more than five elections within his or her lifetime under this subsection.” — Fla. Stat. §318.14(9)

Electing school means adjudication is withheld, no points go on your record, and your civil penalty is reduced by 18%.

How do I check how many I've already used?

There is no free self-service page that shows your election count. Anyone who tells you otherwise is guessing. The count lives on your official driving record, which you have to order.

Option 1 — Order your official driving record (the reliable way)

A 3-year or 7-year record from the Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles lists prior course completions and elections. There is a small state fee.

Which tickets can't be handled with traffic school?

Florida law excludes certain violations from election entirely. If yours is on this list, a course won't remove the points — you need the court.

SituationCan you elect?
Ordinary speeding, under 30 mph over the limitYes
Speeding 30 mph or more over the posted limitNo
You hold a CDL or commercial learner's permitNo
You were driving a commercial vehicleNo
Running a red light or stop signYes
Careless drivingYes
Reckless driving, DUI, racing, leaving the sceneNo — criminal
Red-light camera notice (§316.0083)N/A — no points
No valid license / no registration / no insuranceDifferent rule*
The 30-mph exclusion and the CDL exclusion both come from §318.14(9), which excludes violations of §316.183(2), §316.187 and §316.189 “when the driver exceeds the posted limit by 30 miles per hour or more,” along with §322.61 and §322.62.

*Proof-of-compliance offenses like driving without a valid license, registration or insurance use a separate election under §318.14(10), capped at three per lifetime, and they're satisfied by showing proof — not by taking a course.

Other questions we get every day

How long do I have to elect?

30 days from the date the ticket was issued. You must notify the clerk of court in the county where you got the citation and pay the fine before you take the course. Miss the window and points go on automatically.

Does electing school make my ticket disappear?

No. The citation stays on your record. What changes is that adjudication is withheld and no points are assessed — which is what affects your insurance and your license status.

Will my insurance still go up?

Usually not from a ticket where school was elected and no points were assessed, but carriers set their own rules. If your renewal is coming up, it's worth having someone re-shop it.

How fast do I get my certificate?

We report your completion to the state electronically, typically within a few business days of you finishing. Your name and license number have to match your license exactly or the state rejects it — which is why we ask you to upload your license rather than type it from memory.

I lost my ticket. Now what?

Check the “I don't have my ticket” box on the enrollment form. We'll pull it from the clerk of court for a $15 records search fee. We do need to know which county issued it — Florida clerks don't share a statewide search.
