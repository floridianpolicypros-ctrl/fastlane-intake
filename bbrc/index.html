How it works

  1. Tap START BAIL NOW and fill in the defendant + your info.
  2. Snap a photo of your driver license (front & back) and a selfie — we auto-fill your details.
  3. Review the 10% premium, sign electronically, and submit.
  4. Complete the card authorization. We confirm and post the bond.
  5. Release is usually 4–12 hours (depends on the jail).
