What the program is

If you check the text-message box on our application, you agree that we may send you SMS or MMS messages about your bail bond. These may include court dates and reminders, release status and jail information, payment reminders and receipts, links to documents that need signing, and replies to questions you send us.

If you separately check the optional promotional box, we may also send occasional messages about our services. That box is optional and is not required for anything.

Consent is not a condition of service

You do not have to agree to text messages to get a bail bond. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchasing any good or service. If you do not consent, we will contact you by phone or email instead, and nothing about your bond changes.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies and depends on your case. Message and data rates may apply. Those charges come from your mobile carrier, not from us. Check your plan if you are unsure.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to cancel. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and then no further texts. You may also call us at (561) 833-4388 or email us and ask to be removed.

Stopping texts does not cancel your bond or any obligation under it. We will simply reach you another way.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message, or call (561) 833-4388. We answer 24 hours a day.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is not guaranteed. Do not rely on a text message alone for a court date — always confirm with the court or with us.

Changing your number

If you give up or change the mobile number you gave us, tell us right away. Numbers get reassigned, and we do not want to send messages about your case to a stranger.

Privacy

We do not sell your phone number. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. See our Privacy Policy for what we collect and why.

Changes

We may update these terms. The version in force when you gave consent is the version recorded with your signed application, and that is the version that governs your consent.
