Think you have a warrant?

🚀 Start a walk-through bond
A warrant does not expire and it does not go away on its own. It sits there until it is served, which usually happens at the worst possible moment: a traffic stop, a job background check, or an early-morning knock at the door.

Handling it on purpose is better

If you turn yourself in with a bond already arranged, you choose the day and time. You arrive, get booked, and the bond is posted immediately. People often call this a walk-through bond.

Compare that to being arrested on a Friday night, when first appearance may not happen until the next morning and you spend the weekend in custody.

What we do first

We check whether a warrant actually exists and what the bond is. Sometimes people are worried about a warrant that was already resolved, and sometimes they find out the amount is far lower than they feared.

Knowing the real answer costs nothing and takes a phone call.

Failure to appear

If a warrant was issued because someone missed a court date, the original bond may have been revoked and the new bond is often higher. The sooner it is addressed, the better it tends to go.

If you are currently out on a bond we wrote and you have missed or are about to miss court, call us immediately. There is usually more we can do before a warrant issues than after.

Talk to a lawyer too

We post bonds. We are not attorneys and we cannot advise you on the charge itself, whether to speak to police, or how to resolve the case.

If you need a referral to a criminal defence attorney in Palm Beach County, ask and we will point you toward one.
